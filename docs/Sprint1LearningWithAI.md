---

### What did I learn

- I used ChatGPT and Claude to produce sample texts. My prompt stated:
Create 9 total prompts for a WPM test and 9 more prompts for a Comprehension Test. 3 
of each should be easy difficulty, 3 of each should be Intermediate difficulty,
and 3 of each should be Expert difficulty. These prompts should be between 800-1200 words.
Harder prompts should included harder grammar and older writing styles. Create these 
or find free-use works online.
- Claude was first, and produced prompts that were under 200 words and did not scale the 
difficulty well, so this was a bust.
- Switching to ChatGPT, this produced much better prompts to the length I specified. 
However, it did not create enough prompts to fit the requirements I set for it. I also 
felt that the difficulty was not scaled entirely right. I will have to reapproach this 
at a later date to expand this.
